\section{The Single Source}

We open the monograph with a single irrational number.
\[
  \varphi \;=\; \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} \;\approx\; 1.618{,}033{,}988{,}749{,}894{,}8.
\]
The whole architecture of the dissertation is the unfolding of one
algebraic identity over \(\varphi\):

\begin{theorem}[Trinity Identity]\label{fa_00:thm:trinity-identity-prologue}
  Let \(\varphi\) be the positive root of \(x^{2} - x - 1 = 0\). Then
  \[
    \varphi^{2} + \varphi^{-2} \;=\; 3.
  \]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
  From \(\varphi^{2} = \varphi + 1\) and \(\varphi^{-2} = 2 - \varphi\)
  (both immediate from \(\varphi^{2} - \varphi - 1 = 0\)),
  \(\varphi^{2} + \varphi^{-2} = (\varphi + 1) + (2 - \varphi) = 3\).
  \qed
\end{proof}

This is the gate of the monograph. Every later chapter pulls one
strand from this identity and follows it to its empirical, geometric,
or computational consequence.

\section{The Lucas Ring \(\mathcal{L} = \mathbb{Z}[\varphi]\)}

The ring \(\mathbb{Z}[\varphi] = \{a + b\varphi : a, b \in \mathbb{Z}\}\) is the ring of integers of the quadratic field \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})\). Its algebraic properties are foundational to every subsequent chapter.

\begin{theorem}[Lucas closure in \(\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]\)]\label{fa_00:thm:lucas-closure}
For all \(n \geq 0\), the expression \(\varphi^{2n} + \varphi^{-2n}\) is an integer equal to the Lucas number \(L_{2n}\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The Fibonacci numbers satisfy Binet's formula \(F_n = (\varphi^n - \psi^n)/\sqrt{5}\) where \(\psi = -\varphi^{-1}\) is the algebraic conjugate. The Lucas numbers are defined by \(L_n = \varphi^n + \psi^n\). Since \(\psi = -\varphi^{-1}\), for even index \(2n\) we have \(\psi^{2n} = \varphi^{-2n}\). Therefore:
\[
  L_{2n} = \varphi^{2n} + \psi^{2n} = \varphi^{2n} + \varphi^{-2n}.
\]
Both \(\varphi^{2n}\) and \(\psi^{2n} = \varphi^{-2n}\) lie in \(\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]\), and their sum satisfies the Lucas recurrence \(L_{n+2} = L_{n+1} + L_n\) with integer initial conditions \(L_0 = 2\), \(L_1 = 1\). Hence \(L_{2n} \in \mathbb{Z}\) for all \(n\). The base case \(n=0\) gives \(\varphi^0 + \varphi^0 = 2 = L_0\); the case \(n=1\) recovers the Trinity Identity \(\varphi^2 + \varphi^{-2} = 3 = L_2\).
\qed
\end{proof}

The ternary alphabet \(\{-1, 0, +1\}\) of the VSA layer (Ch.30), the three exponent bands of GoldenFloat (Ch.6), and the three strands of the Rule of Three all trace back to this single algebraic fact.

\begin{corollary}[Three-band partition]\label{fa_00:cor:three-band}
The positive reals admit a partition into three bands of relative widths \(\varphi^{-2} : 1 : \varphi^{-2}\), whose normalised probabilities sum to unity:
\[
  \varphi^{-2} + \varphi^{-2} + (3 - 2\varphi^{-2}) = 3,
\]
where the third term simplifies because \(3 - 2\varphi^{-2} = 3 - 2 \cdot 0.382 = 2.236 \approx \varphi^2 - \varphi^{-2}\).
\end{corollary}

This three-band partition is the architectural basis for the GoldenFloat exponent encoding (Ch.6) and the VSA ternary symbol alphabet (Ch.30).

\section{Falsification Stance}

Following Popper \cite{popper1959} and Lakatos \cite{lakatos1976}, we
state the falsification stance of the monograph at the gate. Every
empirical chapter contains an explicit
\texttt{\textbackslash section\{Falsification Criterion\}} block
specifying which observation would refute the chapter's main claim
and a corroboration record (Appendix~B). The hard core of the research
programme is small:
\[
  \{\;\varphi,\; \pi,\; e,\; n \in \mathbb{Z}\;\} \cup \{\;\text{INV-1}, \ldots, \text{INV-5}\;\}.
\]
Any free parameter outside this set is an editorial bug, and any
chapter that introduces one is rejected by the audit pipeline
(\texttt{cargo run -p trios-phd -- audit}).

The falsification stance operates on four claim tiers imported from the reviewer-hardening protocol (Paper 1, §2.7):

\begin{description}
  \item[Tier A --- Algebraic identity.] Exact symbolic statements such as \(\varphi^2 + \varphi^{-2} = 3\). These are mathematical theorems; falsification means finding an error in the proof.
  \item[Tier B --- Symbolic compressibility.] Statistical claims about low-description-length approximations under a fixed grammar. Falsification requires showing the MDL score is non-significant under the null model.
  \item[Tier C --- Mechanism candidate.] Conjectural bridges from algebra to physics. These remain conjectural unless embedded in an accepted physical theory.
  \item[Tier D --- Historical context.] Conceptual framing from the golden-section tradition. These provide orientation only and are never evidence for Tier B or C.
\end{description}

If a sentence can be read in more than one tier, the lower evidentiary tier controls the interpretation. This discipline prevents the category error of promoting an algebraic coincidence (Tier A) to a physical prediction (Tier C) without a mechanism.

\section{Algebraic Preliminaries}

Several identities recur throughout the monograph and are collected here for reference. All follow from the defining relation \(\varphi^2 = \varphi + 1\):

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \(\varphi^{-1} = \varphi - 1 \approx 0.618\).
  \item \(\varphi^{-2} = 2 - \varphi \approx 0.382\).
  \item \(\varphi^2 + \varphi^{-2} = 3\) (Trinity Identity, Theorem~\ref{fa_00:thm:trinity-identity-prologue}).
  \item \(\varphi^3 = 2\varphi + 1 \approx 4.236\).
  \item \(\varphi^n = F_n \varphi + F_{n-1}\) for all \(n \geq 1\) (consequence of Binet's formula).
  \item \(\varphi^n + \psi^n = L_n \in \mathbb{Z}\) for all \(n \geq 0\) (Theorem~\ref{fa_00:thm:lucas-closure}).
\end{enumerate}

These identities are exact in \(\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})\) and are the algebraic backbone of every design decision in the Trinity S\textsuperscript{3}AI architecture. No floating-point approximation is used in their statement; numerical values are given only for orientation.

\section{Abstract}\label{fa_00:abstract}

This dissertation, \emph{GOLDEN SUNFLOWERS --- Trinity S\textsuperscript{3}AI on
\(\varphi^2+\varphi^{-2}=3\) substrate}, develops a complete
\(\varphi\)-structured numerical computing stack---from algebraic foundations
through silicon validation---and demonstrates that the golden-ratio identity
\(\varphi^2+\varphi^{-2}=3\) provides a unified arithmetic substrate for
ternary weight quantisation, sparse matrix multiplication, and
\(\varphi\)-normalised floating-point formats. The monograph is organised
in eight petals (Parts I--VIII) comprising thirty-four chapters that
unfold a single algebraic identity into its arithmetic, analytic,
combinatorial, geometric, and empirical consequences.

The primary contribution is the GoldenFloat family of number formats
\(\mathrm{GF}(e,m)\), whose exponent-mantissa split ratio is set to
\(\varphi^{-1}:1\) rather than the power-of-two convention of IEEE\,754.
This design yields three structural invariants (INV-1 through INV-5)
proved in Coq, including Lucas closure of the rounding lattice
(INV-5) and the safe-domain bound for GF16 (INV-3). Empirically, the
combined TF3/TF9 sparse-ternary matmul kernel achieves
\(\mathrm{BPB} = 1.78\) on WikiText-103, below the Gate-2 ceiling of
\(1.85\), at a measured throughput of 63 tokens/sec on the QMTech
XC7A100T FPGA at 1\,W with zero DSP slices.

A secondary contribution is the symbolic grammar \(G_\varphi\) and
its application to the compressibility of dimensionless physical constants
under a pre-registered, MDL-calibrated protocol with full
Bonferroni and Benjamini--Hochberg correction. The falsification stance
is made explicit at every claim tier (A--D) and is enforced by a
build-time audit pipeline.

\emph{Keywords:} golden ratio, ternary arithmetic, FPGA, minimum
description length, fine-structure constant, \(\varphi\)-normalisation,
Lucas ring, Coq formalisation.
