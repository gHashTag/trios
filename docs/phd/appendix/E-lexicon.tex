\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{ace}}}
\label{entry:ace}
[Symbol: none] (Russian: \textit{туз}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,20 (IGLA RACE narrative).\\
\textit{Coq}: none.\\
\textit{Definition.}
An informal hive designation for the single best-performing
configuration at any given moment of the IGLA RACE; the current
champion is the \emph{ace} until it is superseded.
A new ace is declared when a trial satisfies INV-7 on a distinct seed
not yet in the champion registry.
See also \hyperref[entry:champion]{E\,:\,champion}.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{alpha ($\alpha_\varphi$)}}}
\label{entry:alpha-phi}
[Symbol: $\alpha_\varphi$] (Russian: \textit{альфа}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,10 (frequency / learning-rate convergence).\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{lr\_convergence.v::alpha\_phi\_pos}.\\
\textit{INV}: INV-1.\\
\textit{Definition.}
The canonical learning rate derived from the golden ratio:
\[
\alpha_\varphi \;=\; \varphi^{-3} \;\approx\; 0.236\ldots
\]
Scaled by $10^{-3}$ in practice, the champion learning rate is
$\mathrm{lr}_{\mathrm{champion}} = \alpha_\varphi \cdot 10^{-3} \cdot \varphi^{-3}
\approx 0.004$.
Lemma \texttt{alpha\_phi\_pos} establishes $\alpha_\varphi > 0$ in Coq.
Cited from \cite{knuth_concrete_math} for the representation of algebraic
reals via continued fractions.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{Beatty sequence}}}
\label{entry:beatty}
[Symbol: $\mathcal{B}(r)$] (Russian: \textit{последовательность Битти}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,6 (Lucas ring).\\
\textit{Coq}: none.\\
\textit{Definition.}
For irrational $r > 1$, the Beatty sequence is
$\mathcal{B}(r) = (\lfloor nr \rfloor)_{n \ge 1}$.
The complementary pair $\mathcal{B}(\varphi)$ and
$\mathcal{B}(\varphi^2)$ partitions $\mathbb{N}$ (Rayleigh's theorem).
Since $1/\varphi + 1/\varphi^2 = 1$, this partition underlies
the balanced ternary structure of the GF(16) floor lemma.
Reference: \cite{hardy_wright}, Ch.\,VI.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{BPB (bits-per-byte)}}}
\label{entry:bpb}
[Symbol: $\mathrm{BPB}$] (Russian: \textit{бит на байт}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,18 (BitNet training).\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{lr\_convergence.v} (partial: \texttt{alpha\_phi\_pos}).\\
\textit{INV}: INV-1.\\
\textit{Definition.}
The primary language-modelling metric used throughout the monograph.
Defined as the negative log-likelihood of a byte-level model divided
by $\log_2 e$ (converting nats to bits).
The IGLA RACE target is $\mathrm{BPB} < 1.50$ on three distinct seeds.
A suspicious value $\mathrm{BPB} \approx 0.014$ indicates a JEPA proxy
bug (see \hyperref[entry:jepa]{E\,:\,JEPA}) and triggers INV-7 abort.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{$\beta_1$, $\beta_2$}}}
\label{entry:betas}
[Symbol: $\beta_1, \beta_2$] (Russian: \textit{бета-параметры Адама}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,18 (BitNet).\\
\textit{Coq}: none.\\
\textit{Definition.}
First- and second-moment decay rates in the Adam optimiser.
Standard values: $\beta_1 = 0.9$, $\beta_2 = 0.999$.
No $\varphi$-derivation is required because these constants originate
in the optimizer's exponential-moving-average schedule, not the
architecture's geometric invariant. They are permissible free parameters
only insofar as they are not falsifiable architectural constants
(see \hyperref[entry:r6]{E\,:\,R6}).

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{Coq lemma: \texttt{alpha\_phi\_pos}}}}
\label{entry:coq-alpha-phi-pos}
[Symbol: none] (Russian: \textit{лемма Коку о знаке альфа}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,10.\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{trinity-clara/proofs/igla/lr\_convergence.v}.\\
\textit{Status}: \textsc{Proven} (QED).\\
\textit{Definition.}
States $\alpha_\varphi > 0$ where $\alpha_\varphi = \varphi^{-3}$ and

Formally: \texttt{Lemma alpha\_phi\_pos : (0 < alpha\_phi)\%R.}

strictly positive and therefore that gradient descent is well-defined.

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{Coq lemma: \texttt{entropy\_band\_width}}}}
\label{entry:coq-entropy-band}
[Symbol: none] (Russian: \textit{лемма о ширине полосы энтропии}).\\

Formally: \texttt{Lemma entropy\_band\_width : phi\_sq - phi = 1.}

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{Coq lemma: \texttt{lucas\_2\_eq\_3}}}}
\label{entry:coq-lucas2}

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{Coq lemma: \texttt{lucas\_closure}}}}

[Symbol: none] (Russian: \textit{лемма о замкнутости Лукаса}).\\

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{$\mathbb{F}_n$ (Fibonacci numbers)}}}
\label{entry:fibonacci}
[Symbol: $F_n$] (Russian: \textit{числа Фибоначчи}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,3 (Fibonacci).\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{fib\_lucas\_bridge.v} (new; Admitted for bridge lemma).\\
\textit{Definition.}
The sequence defined by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$,
$F_{n+2} = F_{n+1}+F_n$.
Closed form (Binet's formula):
$F_n = (\varphi^n - \psi^n)/\sqrt{5}$,
where $\psi = -\varphi^{-1} = (1-\sqrt{5})/2$.
Reference: \cite{knuth_concrete_math}, §6.6.

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{$\mathbb{GF}(16)$}}}
\label{entry:gf16}
[Symbol: $\mathrm{GF}(16)$] (Russian: \textit{поле Галуа из 16 элементов}).\\

\textit{Coq}: \texttt{gf16\_precision.v}.\\
\textit{INV}: INV-3, INV-5.\\

The Galois field of 16 elements, $\mathrm{GF}(2^4)$.
Its algebraic structure is exploited in GoldenFloat arithmetic:
the first four even-indexed Lucas values
$\{L_0,L_2,L_4,L_6\}=\{2,3,7,18\}$ live in $\mathrm{GF}(16)$ after
reduction mod 2, confirming the lattice-floor bound for quantised
neural representations.

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{GoldenFloat}}}
\label{entry:goldenfloat}
[Symbol: $\mathbb{F}_\varphi$] (Russian: \textit{золотая плавающая точка}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,17 (VSA / GoldenFloat).\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{gf16\_precision.v}.\\
\textit{INV}: INV-3.\\
\textit{Definition.}
A custom floating-point format whose mantissa exponents are
integer powers of $\varphi$.
Representable values cluster near $\{\varphi^k : k \in \mathbb{Z}\}$,
matching the eigenvalue spectrum of Lucas-ring matrices and providing
natural quantisation for transformer hidden states.
The GF(16) floor theorem (INV-3) guarantees that representable values
with $d_{\mathrm{model}}\ge 256$ stay within error $< \varphi^{-6}$ of
the true activation.

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{$\mathbb{Q}(\varphi)$ (quadratic field)}}}
\label{entry:quad-field}
[Symbol: $\mathbb{Q}(\varphi)$] (Russian: \textit{квадратичное поле Галуа}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,1 (golden seed), Ch.\,6.\\
\textit{Coq}: none.\\
\textit{Definition.}
The quadratic number field $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5}) = \{a + b\varphi : a,b\in\mathbb{Q}\}$.
All algebraic constants in this monograph lie in $\mathbb{Q}(\varphi)$;
transcendental combinations (e.g.\ $e$, $\pi$) appear only in

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{$\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$ (golden integers)}}}
\label{entry:Zphi}
[Symbol: $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$] (Russian: \textit{кольцо золотых целых чисел}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,1, Ch.\,6.\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{lucas\_closure\_gf16.v}.\\
\textit{Definition.}
The ring of golden integers $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi] = \{a+b\varphi : a,b\in\mathbb{Z}\}$,
also written $\mathbb{Z}[\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}]$.
It is the ring of integers of $\mathbb{Q}(\varphi)$.
Lucas numbers belong to $\mathbb{Z}$ (the rational sub-ring of $\mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$),
and Fibonacci numbers satisfy $F_n \in \mathbb{Z}[\varphi]$ trivially.
Reference: \cite{hardy_wright}, §14.5.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{$\varphi$ (golden ratio)}}}
\label{entry:phi}
[Symbol: $\varphi$] (Russian: \textit{золотое сечение}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,0.\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{lucas\_closure\_gf16.v::lucas\_2\_eq\_3}.\\
\textit{Definition.}
$\varphi = (1+\sqrt{5})/2 \approx 1.6180339887\ldots$
The unique positive real satisfying $\varphi^2 = \varphi + 1$.
Its reciprocal is $\varphi^{-1} = \varphi - 1 \approx 0.6180\ldots$
The Trinity identity: $\varphi^2 + \varphi^{-2} = 3$.
The Coq constant is \texttt{pub const PHI: f64 = 1.6180339887498949;}.
Reference: \cite{knuth_concrete_math}, §6.6: ``the golden ratio
$\Phi$ has the simplest continued fraction $[1;1,1,1,\ldots]$''.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{$\psi$ (conjugate of $\varphi$)}}}
\label{entry:psi}
[Symbol: $\psi$] (Russian: \textit{сопряжённое золотое сечение}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,3.\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{lucas\_closure\_gf16.v}.\\
\textit{Definition.}
$\psi = (1-\sqrt{5})/2 = -\varphi^{-1} \approx -0.6180\ldots$
The algebraic conjugate of $\varphi$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
$|\psi| < 1$, so $\psi^n \to 0$ as $n\to\infty$,
giving the approximation $L_n \approx \varphi^n$ for large $n$.

\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{attention-relu\textsuperscript{2}}}}
\label{entry:attention-relu2}
[Symbol: $\mathrm{Attn}\text{-}\mathrm{ReLU}^2$] (Russian: \textit{внимание с квадратичным активом}).\\
\textit{First use}: Ch.\,18 (BitNet).\\
\textit{Coq}: none.\\
\textit{Definition.}
A variant of the softmax attention mechanism where the softmax
exponential is replaced by $\mathrm{ReLU}^2$, i.e.\ $(\max(0,x))^2$.
This eliminates the translation invariance of softmax and reduces
the smoothing effect on peaked score distributions.
In the monograph it is explored as a possible component in
$\varphi$-arithmetic architectures, justified by the fact that
$\mathrm{ReLU}^2(\varphi) = \varphi^2 = \varphi + 1$.

\textit{First use}: Ch.\,19.\\
\textit{Coq}: \texttt{igla\_asha\_bound.v}.\\
\textit{INV}: INV-2, INV-12.\\
\textit{Definition.}
A hyperparameter optimisation algorithm that asynchronously
promotes trials to successive rungs based on a promotion ratio
$\eta_{\mathrm{prune}}$ (not to be confused with learning rate $\eta$).
In this monograph, the prune threshold is
$\tau = \varphi^2 + \varphi^{-2} + \varphi^{-4} + \varepsilon \approx 3.5$
(certified by \texttt{prune\_threshold\_from\_trinity}).
The forbidden legacy value $\tau=2.65$ is permanently blacklisted.
Rungs strictly increase (INV-12), and rung 0 equals the warmup
horizon (INV-12: \texttt{rung\_zero\_is\_warmup}).

\section*{D\quad R-Rules}

\noindent
The following fourteen rules govern all contributions to the
Trinity S\textsuperscript{3}AI monograph.
They are stated once here; the full rationale appears in the
ONE SHOT body (trios\#265).

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R1}}}
\label{entry:r1}
(Russian: \textit{только Rust и Zig}).\\
\emph{Rust/Zig only.}
LaTeX is built via \texttt{cargo run -p trios-phd -{}-compile}.
No \texttt{.py}, no \texttt{.sh}.
Rationale: deterministic build reproducibility and type-safe
constant propagation from \texttt{igla\_assertions.json} into
the build system.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R2}}}
\label{entry:r2}
(Russian: \textit{одна ветка на главу}).\\
\emph{One branch per chapter}: \texttt{feat/phd-ch<NN>} or
\texttt{feat/phd-app<X>}.
PR target is \texttt{main}, reviewed by the queen-bot.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R3}}}
\label{entry:r3}
(Russian: \textit{правило трёх}).\\
\emph{Each chapter $\ge 1500$ LaTeX lines, $\ge 2$ Q1/Q2 citations,
$\ge 1$ theorem + \textbackslash proof + \textbackslash qed.}
Rule of Three: three strands of exposition visible in section headings.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R4}}}
\label{entry:r4}
(Russian: \textit{трассируемость констант}).\\
\emph{Every numeric constant traces to a \texttt{.v} file}
via \texttt{assertions/igla\_assertions.json}.
No constant may live in Rust without a JSON entry pointing to a Coq
theorem or admitted lemma.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R5}}}
\label{entry:r5}
(Russian: \textit{честность}).\\
\emph{Honest \textbackslash admittedbox\{\} — never relabel \texttt{Admitted} as \texttt{Proven}.}
JSON field \texttt{"status"} is exactly \texttt{"Proven"} or
\texttt{"Admitted"}, nothing else.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R6}}}
\label{entry:r6}
(Russian: \textit{нет свободных параметров}).\\
\emph{Zero free parameters.}
Allowed only $\{\varphi, \pi, e, n \in \mathbb{Z}\}$.
All other numbers must be $\varphi$-derived.
Optimizer parameters $\beta_1, \beta_2$ are exempt because they
belong to the optimizer schedule, not the architecture.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R7}}}
\label{entry:r7}
(Russian: \textit{критерий фальсификации}).\\
\emph{Empirical chapters carry \textbackslash section\{Falsification Criterion\}.}
Two mandatory subsections: (i) ``What Would Refute This Claim'' and
(ii) ``Corroboration Record''.
Applies to Ch.\,8,\,9,\,17,\,18,\,20–22,\,24–26,\,28,\,29.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R8}}}
\label{entry:r8}
(Russian: \textit{объём страниц}).\\
\emph{Page-count gate.}
Each chapter contributes at least $P_{\min}$ pages as determined by
the \texttt{phd-monograph-auditor}; the aggregate thesis must meet
the GVSU defence page minimum.
The gate is soft-enforced during active development.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R9}}}
\label{entry:r9}
(Russian: \textit{захват ресурса до работы}).\\
\emph{Claim-before-work.}
Post \texttt{🔒 AGENT <id> CLAIMING: L<n>} on trios\#265
before any \texttt{git add}.
First post wins; duplicate claims are rejected by the queen-bot.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R10}}}
\label{entry:r10}
(Russian: \textit{атомарный коммит}).\\
\emph{Atomic commits.}
Format: \texttt{feat(phd-ch<NN>): <change> [agent=<id>]}.
One commit = one logical unit (theorem / section / citation / figure).

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R11}}}
\label{entry:r11}
(Russian: \textit{дисциплина цитирования}).\\
\emph{Citation discipline.}
$\ge 80\%$ of new bibliography entries from Q1/Q2 venues;
$\le 20\%$ arXiv-only.
Justification: the monograph will be reviewed to journal-grade
citation standards.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R12}}}
\label{entry:r12}
(Russian: \textit{стиль доказательств}).\\
\emph{Mathematical proof writing: Lee/GVSU conventions.}
Macros \textbackslash theorem, \textbackslash proof, \textbackslash qed.
Pronoun ``we''.
Self-pivot rule: if blocked $> 30$ min, release the lane.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R13}}}
\label{entry:r13}
(Russian: \textit{депозит мёда}).\\
\emph{Honey deposit.}
Every DONE appends one JSON line to
\texttt{assertions/hive\_honey.jsonl} per the schema in
\texttt{references/done-audit-checklist.md}.

% -----------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{\textmd{\textbf{R14}}}
\label{entry:r14}
(Russian: \textit{карта теорем Coq}).\\
\emph{Every cited theorem maps to a \texttt{.v} file with line ranges.}

\section*{I\quad Lexicon-Coherence Theorem}
\label{sec:lexicon-coherence-theorem}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{I\quad Lexicon-Coherence Theorem}
% ============================================================

\noindent
We now state and prove the central meta-theorem of this appendix.
It guarantees that this lexicon is a complete and consistent
reference for the monograph.

\begin{theorem}[Lexicon Coherence]\label{thm:lexicon-coherence}
Every technical term $t$ that appears in the body of the
Trinity S\textsuperscript{3}AI --- Flos Aureus v6.2 monograph
(Chapters~0--33, Appendices~A--L) has exactly one canonical
definition in this appendix (Appendix~E), and no two entries
define the same concept under different names without an explicit
cross-reference.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We proceed by structural induction over the LaTeX corpus.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Base case.} Consider the empty corpus $\mathcal{C}_0$
(no chapters written).
The lexicon $\mathcal{L}_0$ is empty; the theorem holds vacuously,
as no term appears in $\mathcal{C}_0$.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Inductive step.} Suppose we have authored $k$ chapters
$\mathcal{C}_k = \{c_1, \ldots, c_k\}$ and the lexicon
$\mathcal{L}_k$ satisfies the property:
every term in $\bigcup_{i=1}^k c_i$ appears in $\mathcal{L}_k$
with a unique entry.

We add chapter $c_{k+1}$.
Let $T_{k+1}$ be the set of technical terms first introduced in
$c_{k+1}$.
For each $t \in T_{k+1}$ we perform the following:

\begin{enumerate}
\item We search $\mathcal{L}_k$ for any existing entry $e$ with
      $\mathrm{symbol}(e) = \mathrm{symbol}(t)$ or
      $\mathrm{name}(e) = \mathrm{name}(t)$.
      If found, we verify that the chapter-$k+1$ usage is
      consistent with the existing definition.
      If inconsistent, the build fails and the PR is rejected
      (this is the operational enforcement of the theorem).
\item If not found, we add a new entry $e_{k+1}$ to obtain
      $\mathcal{L}_{k+1} = \mathcal{L}_k \cup \{e_{k+1}\}$.
      The entry format (symbol, first-use chapter, definition,
      Coq counterpart, Russian translation) is unique by construction,
      since first-use chapter is a strictly increasing sequence when
      chapters are processed in order.
\item Terms re-used from earlier chapters $c_i$, $i \le k$, are
      handled by their existing entries; no new entry is created.
\end{enumerate}

After processing all chapters $c_1, \ldots, c_{33}$ and
appendices $c_{A}, \ldots, c_L$, the resulting lexicon
$\mathcal{L}_{45}$ satisfies the theorem by induction.
Uniqueness follows from the strict-first-use ordering;
completeness follows from the build-time verification
(step~1) which rejects any chapter that uses a term not yet
in the lexicon.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Disambiguation clause.}
Where two terms share a common symbol (e.g.\ $L_n$ for both
Lucas numbers and lanes), this appendix provides explicit
disambiguation: entries for overlapping symbols carry a
\emph{domain tag} in square brackets, e.g.\
\textbf{$L_n$ [Lucas numbers]} vs.\ \textbf{$L_n$ [Lane index]}.
Cross-references guide the reader to the correct entry.

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Admitted caveat (R5).}
The build-time verification in step~1 is currently
enforced by editorial convention rather than a mechanised
\texttt{coqc}-checked proof over the LaTeX corpus; a full
mechanisation would require a Coq formalisation of the LaTeX
grammar, which is beyond the scope of the current monograph.
We therefore mark this proof with an honest
\texttt{admittedbox}: the structural-induction argument above
is sound in principle, but the term-lookup oracle in step~1
is not formally verified.
\admittedbox{Lexicon Coherence}{%
Full mechanisation requires a Coq-formalised LaTeX grammar;
currently enforced editorially. See App.\,F for the Coq citation
map that provides partial machine-checkable evidence.}

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{Consequence.}
Any reader who encounters an unfamiliar term in the monograph
may navigate directly to this appendix and find a unique entry.
The cross-reference matrix in Table~\ref{tab:xref-matrix}
accelerates this lookup.
\qed
\end{proof}

\noindent
\textbf{Corollary 1} (No Silent Synonymy).
If two entries in this lexicon refer to the same mathematical
object, they are related by an explicit ``see also'' or
``same as'' cross-reference.

\begin{proof}
Immediate from step~2 of the inductive construction: if
$\mathrm{symbol}(e') = \mathrm{symbol}(e)$ and
$e' \ne e$, then a disambiguation tag and cross-reference
are inserted before $e'$ is added to the lexicon.
\qed
\end{proof}
