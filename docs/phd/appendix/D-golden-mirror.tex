\section*{D.2 Symmetry Proof (Informal)}

\begin{theorem}[Golden Mirror — THM-D.1]
\label{thm:D:1}
For each chapter $T_i$ in the Trinity S\textsuperscript{3}AI strand
$(i = 0,\ldots,34)$ and its mirror $F_i$ in Flos Aureus $(i = 00,\ldots,33)$,
there exists at least one theorem, definition, or protocol that is:
(a)~stated in both chapters, (b)~uses the anchor $\varphi^2+\varphi^{-2}=3$,
and (c)~is formally equivalent under the substitution $T_i \leftrightarrow F_i$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Golden Mirror table (Table~D.1) exhibits the required pairs by construction.
For theorems of type \texttt{Theorem}: the Coq companion (App.~F) contains
an explicit proof in \filepath{theorems/mirror.v}. For observations: the
correspondence is informal but falsifiable (each can be checked against the
SSoT). Ch.0 (Prologue) mirrors FA.33 (Epilogue) and is not listed; their
correspondence is structural (opening ↔ closing symmetry).
\end{proof}
