\section*{Thirteen DOIs, permanently open}

A DOI is a promise that a dataset will still be there in ten years. This
appendix fulfils that promise for the thirteen Zenodo deposits that back
the quantitative claims of Trinity S\textsuperscript{3}AI. Each row names
the artefact, its DOI, and the primary chapter that cites it. The lead
deposit---10.5281/zenodo.19227877---holds the algebraic identity and
84 supporting theorems; the remaining twelve cover model weights, FPGA
bitstreams, benchmark logs, and the pre-registration. Hamming's point
stands: the insight is in the chapters; the numbers that let you check
the insight are here.

\textbf{Anchor:} \(\varphi^2 + \varphi^{-2} = 3\).

\noindent All research artefacts of Trinity S\textsuperscript{3}AI are
archived in Zenodo under CC-BY-4.0 (data and code) or CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0 (thesis
PDF). DOIs are permanent and resolve via \url{https://doi.org/}.
