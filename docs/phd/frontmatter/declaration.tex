\section*{Disclosure of AI-Assisted Tooling}

In accordance with the University of Reading's policy on the use of
generative artificial intelligence in research, the author discloses
the following: large-language-model assistants (Claude, GPT, and
Gemini families) were used as \emph{secretarial} aids during the
preparation of this dissertation, in three specific roles:
(i)~LaTeX typesetting and macro debugging;
(ii)~bibliographic curation against the 212-entry
\filepath{bibliography.bib} master file; and
(iii)~prose-style editing for the Helen Sword / Pinker discipline of
``ordinary words for complex things'' as referenced in the Preface.
No theorem statement, no Coq tactic, no Rust runtime guard, no
empirical claim, and no numerical result was generated by an AI
system. Every formal artefact in
\filepath{trinity-clara/proofs/igla/} and every assertion in
\filepath{assertions/igla\_assertions.json} was authored, audited,
and mechanically verified by the candidate. The honest
\emph{Admitted} markers in the citation map (Appendix~F) are listed
verbatim, without flattering paraphrase.

\section*{Statement of Originality and Reproducibility}

The Trinity Identity $\varphi^{2}+\varphi^{-2}=3$ on which this
monograph is built is, to the candidate's knowledge, original to the
author in its present formulation as a
\emph{theorem-guided architecture search anchor}. Earlier scattered
appearances of the algebraic relation in pure-mathematics folklore are
cited where relevant. The empirical anchors --- in particular the
CoBr$_{2}$ critical-temperature window, the icosahedral
quasicrystal lattice constants, and the IGLA-race convergence at
bits-per-byte $<1.85$ --- are reproducible from the artefacts archived
at Zenodo DOIs
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18947017}{10.5281/zenodo.18947017},
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19227877}{10.5281/zenodo.19227877},
and
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19227879}{10.5281/zenodo.19227879},
and from the ACM Artefact-Evaluation pack documented in
Appendix~H. Any reader with access to a single CUDA-capable GPU and
the published seed list (\texttt{17, 42, 1729}) can reproduce the
headline results within $\pm0.5\,\%$.
