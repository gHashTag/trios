\section*{Erratum E2 --- Honest disclosure of the
Format$\times$Algorithm matrix scope}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Erratum E2 --- Format$\times$Algorithm
matrix scope}

\paragraph{Status.}
This erratum is published in advance of the public defense
(2026-06-15) and Rehearsal~\#2 (2026-05-25). It supersedes the
original completion claim attached to closed issue
\href{https://github.com/gHashTag/trios/issues/446}{gHashTag/trios\#446}
and reframes the matrix-completion narrative in line with the
honest gate hardened in
\href{https://github.com/gHashTag/trios/pull/570}{gHashTag/trios\#570}
(R7-honest closure: $\text{rows}\geq3$, distinct seeds~$\geq2$,
$\max(\text{step})\geq3000$).

\paragraph{What was originally claimed.}
The thesis prospectus and several intermediate technical reports
referred to a complete 312-cell Format$\times$Algorithm sweep
(39~numerical formats $\times$ 8~optimisation algorithms). The
\texttt{ssot.bpb\_samples} table was, at the time of issue~\#446
closure, populated with a single-row stub per cell, which the legacy
closure gate accepted at face value
(\texttt{WHERE bpb > 1.0 GROUP BY format, algo}).

\paragraph{What the audit revealed.}
The lane L-MR-L4 audit of \texttt{matrix\_per\_cell\_audit.csv}
(312 rows, see \texttt{assertions/matrix\_per\_cell\_audit.csv} in
the repository at the time of writing) showed that
\emph{every} cell carried \texttt{synthetic\_flag\_R7=1}, with the
typical reason being \texttt{n\_rows=1<3; distinct\_seeds=1<2}
or worse. In other words, no cell in the original sweep met the
hardened R7 thresholds. The only safe path forward is a partial,
honest re-population on the user's own hardware, governed by a
priority subset that the defense schedule can actually accommodate.

\paragraph{What is being claimed instead.}
For the 2026-06-15 defense and the 2026-05-25 rehearsal, the
honest matrix evidence is restricted to a 50-cell priority subset
selected by the rules below and pinned in
\texttt{assertions/matrix\_priority\_50.csv}. The selection is
deterministic and reproducible from
\texttt{assertions/matrix\_per\_cell\_audit.csv} alone:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Golden formats} --- every cell whose format is in
        the golden set $\{\texttt{gf16}\}$ (cf.\ \texttt{gf16\_fa}
        and \texttt{ternary*} which are listed for completeness in
        the selection rule but are not yet present in the matrix
        baseline).
  \item \textbf{Priority optimisers} --- every cell whose algorithm
        is in $\{\texttt{muon}, \texttt{adamw}\}$ (the two priority
        optimisers actually present in the matrix; \texttt{soap}
        and \texttt{shampoo} from the original priority list are
        not implemented in the audit baseline and are therefore
        deferred).
  \item \textbf{Lowest-bpb decile} --- the ten cells with the
        smallest \texttt{min\_bpb\_neon} (most defensible /
        competitive on prior synthetic baseline).
  \item \textbf{Highest-heterogeneity decile} --- the ten cells
        with the largest \texttt{distinct\_sha\_git} (most
        provenance heterogeneity already on record).
\end{enumerate}

The four layers are applied in order with deduplication and the
result is capped at exactly 50 rows. In the present baseline the
cap is reached during layer~2 (golden + priority), so layers~3
and~4 are documented but not active --- a fact that is recorded
in \texttt{priority\_reason} per row.

\paragraph{Honest population protocol.}
The 50 priority cells are populated by the local runner
\texttt{scripts/run\_priority\_matrix.ts} (TypeScript per the
repository's law L1, which forbids \texttt{.sh} files). Each
cell is run with the three Lucas/Fibonacci seeds
$\{47, 89, 144\}$ for $3000$~training steps each, yielding
$50 \times 3 = 150$ honest sample rows that satisfy the R7-honest
gate by construction:
$\text{rows} = 3 \geq 3$, distinct seeds $= 3 \geq 2$,
$\max(\text{step}) = 3000 \geq 3000$.
The verdict is computed by the dedicated gate
\texttt{.github/scripts/closure\_gate\_priority.py}, which is a
read-only PASS/FAIL tool with cell-by-cell breakdown; it never
posts comments, never closes issues, and never touches GitHub
state. Production closure
(\texttt{.github/scripts/closure\_gate.py}) remains the sole
authority for the full 312-cell grid and is unchanged by this
erratum.

\paragraph{What is deferred.}
The remaining $312 - 50 = 262$ cells of the full sweep are
\emph{deferred} to the post-defense work-package and remain marked
as synthetic in
\texttt{assertions/matrix\_per\_cell\_audit.csv}. The thesis text
will explicitly cite the 50-cell priority result and clearly
delimit it from the deferred cells; no claim of complete
312-cell honest coverage will be made at the viva. Any cell whose
training run fails to converge in $3000$ steps is recorded as
\texttt{status: "non\_convergent"} in
\texttt{assertions/matrix\_samples.jsonl} per the L-MR-L3-ALT
protocol (\href{https://github.com/gHashTag/trios/issues/582}{\#582})
and is excluded from the honest gate rather than being faked.

\paragraph{Rationale for this erratum's existence.}
Per R5 (\emph{honest status}) and R7 (\emph{anti-fake-pass guard})
of the trios laws, every public claim about matrix completeness
must be backed by data that satisfies the same SQL predicate that
the closure gate enforces. The original 312-cell narrative did
not. This erratum restores that property by (i) explicitly
withdrawing the 312-cell completion claim, (ii) substituting the
50-cell priority claim that \emph{is} backed by data once the
local runner has executed, and (iii) listing the deferred 262
cells as future work. The substitution is queen-approved and
issue-tracked, and constitutes the matrix evidence that the
2026-06-15 defense and the 2026-05-25 rehearsal will rely on.
