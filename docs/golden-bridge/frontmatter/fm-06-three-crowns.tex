2. \textbf{Module-density test.} Phi has 51 modules, Euler 90, Gamma 105. The anchor surviving an order-of-magnitude variation in module count argues against accidental constructive interference in the Verilog hierarchy.

The anchor surviving three different algebraic substrates is the Strand-I⟂Strand-II compatibility check.
